# Root Cellar arena mockup (not in the game yet)

`cellar.js` builds the instanced arena under the ruined farm, about 24 by 18 units: an earth floor with a plank area, stone and packed-earth walls with timber posts and joists, a ladder up to the hatch with daylight falling down it, a crumbled tunnel at the far end where the waves come out, crates, barrels, grain sacks, hay and shelves of jars, and six lanterns. A party of four and a wave of monsters show the scale. Paste after `frame();` to look at it. `preview.png` shows it.
