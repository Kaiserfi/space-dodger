# Quarry mockup (not in the game yet)

`quarry.js` builds a working quarry south-west of the smithy (centre x 12, z 72): a levelled gravel floor, stepped cliffs, a timber-shored mine entrance, rails with an ore cart, a hoist, ore and spoil heaps, a pickaxe rack, crates and lamps, and a road in from the smithy road. It moves the ore rocks and the goblin there. To test it, paste it right after `spawnMonster(...)` in `adventure/index.html` and widen the grass mask (`GM.z1`) to 96. `preview.png` shows how it looks.
